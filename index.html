01 — About

A theorist building bridges between simulation and observation.

My work joins large-scale 3D particle-in-cell simulations to multi-mission spacecraft data (THEMIS, Cluster, MMS), with the goal of writing down the kinetic rules that govern reconnection, fronts, and the transport of energy in the Earth’s magnetotail.

/ 01

What I study

Kinetic plasma processes — magnetic reconnection, dipolarization fronts, jet-driven instabilities — and the way energy partitions between ions and electrons across many decades of scale.

/ 02

How I work

Theory-guided 3D PIC simulations on HPC clusters, reproducible Python / HDF5 analysis pipelines, and publication-quality diagnostics designed to match what spacecraft can actually measure.

02 — Featured Simulation

3D kinetic reconnection, captured frame by frame.

A volume-rendered slice from a recent particle-in-cell run resolving electron-scale current sheets, flux ropes, and the corrugation of a dipolarization front as it propagates earthward. Press play to watch the front evolve.

Method

3D PIC, open-boundary tail model, m_i/m_e = 100, run on VSCA Tier-1.

What you’re seeing

Earthward flow channel forming a steepening front; flux-rope birth at the X-line; electron-scale corrugation.

Why it matters

Front structuring and partitioning of J·E set how reconnection energy reaches the inner magnetosphere.

03 — Research

Four threads, one question: how do kinetic plasmas convert energy?

Ongoing projects span the X-line, the dipolarization front behind it, the instabilities that structure it, and the diagnostic frameworks that quantify dissipation across scales.

/ 01 — Reconnection

Magnetic reconnection in the magnetotail

3D kinetic reconnection, flux ropes, Hall physics, and the multiscale structure of exhausts and fronts under realistic plasma-sheet conditions.

3D PICFlux ropesHall
/ 02 — Fronts

Dipolarization fronts and jet dynamics

Formation, steepening, and corrugation of fronts; coupling to mesoscale bursty bulk flows and the bundles of transient transport behind them.

DFs / DFBsBBFsMesoscale
/ 03 — Instabilities

Instabilities and turbulence at fronts

Ballooning / interchange, lower-hybrid drift, whistler activity — and their role in transport, mixing, and the breakdown of MHD-scale order.

BallooningLHDIWhistlers
/ 04 — Dissipation

Energy conversion and dissipation proxies

Diagnostics based on J·E, pressure-tensor work, and field–particle correlations — built to be testable against MMS-class observations.
